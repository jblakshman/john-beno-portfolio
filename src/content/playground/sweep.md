Made while thinking about the Orient redesign. The way a seconds hand moves tells you what drives the watch. A quartz watch ticks once per second. A mechanical movement beating at 28,800 vibrations per hour moves in eight small steps every second. Some movements glide without steps at all.

The dial always shows the current time in Munich. Dragging around it moves the clock by an hour per full turn.
