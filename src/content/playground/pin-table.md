A small study for the Tactical Haptic Twin, where a physical city model changes shape under the commander’s hands. Here every pin is a spring that follows a target height: a soft bump around the pointer, a slow ambient wave, and ripples that travel outward when you press.

Built with the Canvas 2D API. Pins are drawn back to front so nearer ones overlap the ones behind them.
