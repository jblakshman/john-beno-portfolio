Mona Sans is a variable font with two axes: weight from 200 to 900 and width from 75 to 125. Each letter measures its distance to the pointer and eases toward a new weight and width, so the words swell around you like a magnifier made of type.

When the pointer is away, a slow wave moves through the letters instead.
