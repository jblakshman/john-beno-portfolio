## Context

Start with the situation, not the solution. Who was involved, what was going wrong for them and why it mattered. Two short paragraphs are enough. Someone who reads only this section and the "At a glance" card above should understand the project.

Then say what you were asked to do, or what you set out to do, and what constrained you: time, technology, access to users, a brief.
