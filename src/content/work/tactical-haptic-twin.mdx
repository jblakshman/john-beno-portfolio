## Brief

For this project, I focused on the intersection of physical presence and digital intelligence to create a future-forward command concept for the fire department. I wanted to move beyond the limitations of flat screens and purely virtual data to create a system that feels grounded and intuitive. I call this concept the Tactical Haptic Twin.

The core idea is to merge a physical, shape-shifting 3D map of the city with an augmented reality digital twin. In this hybrid setup, the physical model acts as the stage, providing a tangible, stable anchor for infrastructure and terrain, while the AR layer acts as the actors, visualizing dynamic real-time data like fire spread, smoke flow and unit positions.

## Idea

My goal was to design an interface that reduces cognitive load during high-stress situations. By using tangible user interfaces, commanders don’t just look at a map, they interact with it. Moving physical tokens on the table translates directly into digital commands for real-world units. This creates a loop where the digital world informs the physical, and physical actions control the digital.

It represents a shift from simply displaying information to creating a feelable access point for complex data. Ultimately, this concept is about using design to bring clarity to chaos, so that in critical moments technology serves human intuition rather than overwhelming it.
