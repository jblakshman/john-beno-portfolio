Notes are the small things: a technique you figured out, a detail from a project that did not fit the case study, a reaction to something you read or built. A few hundred words is plenty. They show how you think between projects, and they give search engines more reasons to connect your name with the work you do.

## A good note

- Has one point, stated in the first two sentences
- Shows something: a sketch, a screen recording, a snippet of code
- Ends when the point is made

## Publishing

Copy this file, rename it (the file name becomes the address: `/notes/file-name`), change the date and set `draft: false`. Notes appear on the live site once `notes` is switched on in `src/site.config.ts`. The newest ones also go out in the RSS feed at `/rss.xml`.
